Output: `output/holy_spirit_sample_60s.mp4` (1920×1080, 30 fps, narration from `audio/holy_spirit_first_60s.mp3`).

## 1. Reference style and what the code reproduces

| Element | Reference | In this sample |
|---|---|---|
| Characters | White, faceless "mask" heads; thick dark outlines; grey shading on one side; robes with folds; body-language acting | **Reproduced.** Generated SVG figures with posable arms and head tilt, folds and one-sided shading. A hand-drawn wobble filter roughens the lines. |
| Our mascot | (none: we don't copy theirs) | **Father**: black cassock with buttons, sash, Roman collar tab and a gold pectoral cross; dark side-parted hair; same faceless mask head. |
| Backgrounds | Painterly, single-hue, layered hills, foliage, clouds, cities; far layers blurred | **Approximated.** Code-drawn layered hills, mountains, towns, trees and clouds, with painted colour blotches, grass strokes and depth blur. They look cleaner and more "vector" than a hand-painted background. |
| Text | Big bold white titles with soft shadow; white bar charts and arrows | **Reproduced.** Montserrat Black/ExtraBold with soft shadows; words pop in one by one as they are spoken. |
| Motion | Slow pushes and pans with parallax, whip-pan blur, pop and slide-ins, colour strips, swarms of "?" | **Reproduced.** All of these are used. |
| Not possible in code | Hand-animated limbs and cloth; frame-by-frame drawn acting | **Approximated** with posed arm rigs, bobbing walks, breathing and floating. |

## Sources for on-screen text
- "Did you receive the Holy Spirit…?" / "We have never even heard that there is a Holy Spirit." are Acts 19:2 (RSV-CE). The first is shortened exactly as the narration says it; the full verse ends "…when you believed?".
- Sign of the Cross formula: Matthew 28:19; CCC 2157.
- "the Lord, the giver of life": Nicene-Constantinopolitan Creed (Roman Missal).
- Third person of the Trinity, fully God: CCC 253–255, 685.
- "Counselor": John 14:16, 14:26 (RSV-CE).

## Rebuilding
```
cd engine
node preview.js 3.0 10.5 22        # PNG stills -> engine/pv/
node render.js 0 1800 30 out.mp4 4 # frames [0,1800) with 4 parallel browsers
```
Then mux: `ffmpeg -i out.mp4 -i ../audio/holy_spirit_first_60s.mp3 -c:v copy -c:a aac -b:a 192k -shortest final.mp4`.
`index.html` is the engine, `art.js` the drawing library, `scenes.js` the timeline, and `T.js` the word timings plus the `W()` helper.
